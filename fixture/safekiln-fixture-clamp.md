# safekiln-fixture: pinned stack

Held through its mounting holes, to +/-0.479 mm.

| Pin | Role | Shank | Thread | Shoulder | Head recess | Sleeve set |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | hole H1 | 3 mm | M2.5 | 20 mm | 5.13 mm | - |
| P2 | hole H2 | 3 mm | M2.5 | 20 mm | 5.13 mm | - |
| P3 | hole H3 | 3 mm | M2.5 | 20 mm | 5.13 mm | - |
| P4 | hole H4 | 3 mm | M2.5 | 20 mm | 5.13 mm | - |

Sleeves, cut to length:

- P1 plate-guide: bore 3.1, outside 5.1, 1.57 mm long
- P1 guide-dut: bore 3.1, outside 5.1, 2.4 mm long
- P2 plate-guide: bore 3.1, outside 5.1, 1.57 mm long
- P2 guide-dut: bore 3.1, outside 5.1, 2.4 mm long
- P3 plate-guide: bore 3.1, outside 5.1, 1.57 mm long
- P3 guide-dut: bore 3.1, outside 5.1, 2.4 mm long
- P4 plate-guide: bore 3.1, outside 5.1, 1.57 mm long
- P4 guide-dut: bore 3.1, outside 5.1, 2.4 mm long

Assembly:

1. Lay the probe plate on the printed nest. Heat each pin and press it down through the plate's own hole into its bore until the head is home in its recess. The plate is the jig: it decides where the pins end up.
2. Let them cool with the plate on. Check each pin square with a small square.
3. Leave the plate on. Fit the lower sleeves, the guide plate, then the upper sleeves.
4. Per board: lower it over the pins, washer and thumb nut, finger-tight.
