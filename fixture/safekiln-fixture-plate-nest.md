# safekiln-fixture-plate-nest

108.2 x 94.1 x 9.5 mm. Print it the right way up, no supports, ASA, four perimeters round the pin seats.

4 pin seats. Each bore is undersized against its pin so the pin is melted in, and the head's recess opens on the underside.

## Mounting it to a rack shelf

4 M3 heat-set inserts in the underside, 4 mm holes 6.2 mm deep. Press them in with the iron after printing, flush with the underside, and screw the nest down from below through the shelf.

Drill the shelf at these centres. Seen from above with the connector edge nearest you, they are measured from the far left corner: x to the right, y towards you.

- R1: x 4 mm, y 4 mm
- R2: x 104.2 mm, y 4 mm
- R3: x 4 mm, y 90.1 mm
- R4: x 104.2 mm, y 90.1 mm
